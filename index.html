Activity 5: Commuting in Large Cities (B2 Level)

Key Vocabulary:

  • 1. Commuter: A person who travels some distance to work on a regular basis.
  • 2. Congestion: The state of being crowded and full of traffic.
  • 3. Infrastructure: The basic physical systems of a city (roads, railways).
  • 4. Pedestrian: A person walking along a road or in a developed area.
  • 5. Public transit: Buses, trains, and other forms of transport available to the public.
  • 6. Rush hour: The times of day when traffic is at its heaviest.
  • 7. Sustainable: Able to be maintained at a certain rate or level.
  • 8. Urbanization: The process of making an area more urban (city-like).
  • 9. Gridlock: A traffic jam so bad that no movement is possible.
  • 10. Emission: The production and discharge of something, especially gas.

Part A: Drag and drop (or tap to select, then tap the gap) the relative clauses to complete the text.
